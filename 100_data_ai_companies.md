## Notes

**Highest total comp for Data/AI Engineers right now:**
OpenAI, Anthropic, Netflix, Meta, and quant firms like Citadel/Jane Street/Two Sigma tend to top compensation surveys — often significantly above "big tech" median once you include RSUs/bonuses.

**Best pay-to-effort ratio in India:**
GCCs (Goldman, JPMorgan, Walmart Global Tech, Visa/Mastercard) often match or beat Indian product companies for senior data/AI roles, plus better stability.

**Fastest-growing demand:**
AI infra/tooling companies (Databricks, Snowflake, Scale AI) and AI-native labs are hiring aggressively for MLOps, data platform, and applied AI engineering roles.
